The LPSU (short for Low-Power Power Supply Unit) is a power supply unit that delivers constant power to all the electronics, in the form of a regulated 5V/2.5A low power rail. 
Simultaneously, it can handle 1S2P lithium batteries charge from a high voltage input delivered by our ground equipment, or from a terminal block for unit testing purposes.

This is the revised version of the LPSU that flew on Firehorn in October 2025 and brings the following improvements:
* Battery protection controller
    - Under/overvoltage protection
    - Reverse polarity protection
    - Overcurrent protection
 * 4-phase boost converter
 * Exhaustive status indicators and test points
